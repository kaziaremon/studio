Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear, practical answers about how we work, budgets, timelines, and account security.

Paid advertising starts driving traffic right away, but steady profitability takes real testing. During the first 7 to 10 days, we test different visual hooks, audience segments, and messaging angles to see what generates the lowest cost per lead or sale. Once the winning combinations are clear, we optimize and scale them. Realistically, expect baseline performance in the first two weeks, with dependable, stable results developing between weeks 3 and 4.
You do not need a huge budget to begin. We recommend starting with a manageable testing budget that allows Meta or LinkedIn to collect enough daily data without straining your cash flow. You pay your ad spend directly to the advertising platforms from your own company payment card because we never mark up or hold your ad dollars. Once we prove a positive return on spend, you can increase your budget with total confidence.
No honest agency can guarantee exact revenue numbers because closed sales also depend on factors outside of advertising, such as your product pricing, market demand, website checkout experience, and how fast your internal team follows up with new leads. What we do guarantee is disciplined management, which means testing ads thoroughly, tracking every conversion clearly, stopping wasted spend immediately, and treating your budget as carefully as our own.
When potential customers see your ad, a large percentage of them tap your profile to check your credibility before buying. If your bio is unclear, your contact links are missing, or your story highlights look abandoned, they leave without taking action. Social Media Optimization cleans up your profile so that the visitors you already paid for actually trust you and convert into paying clients.
You own everything completely. All campaigns run inside your own Meta Business Manager and LinkedIn Campaign Manager. You simply add Whiz Studio as an agency partner. You keep full administrative control, card payment security, and complete customer data history at all times. If you ever pause or finish working with us, all assets and campaign data remain with you.
